# Film craft

## Story for short films
- One character, one want, one problem. Link beats with "therefore" and "but", not "and then".
- 15-60 s: a hook in the first 20%, 2-3 beats of escalation, a turn by the midpoint, and a payoff at the end that changes the meaning of the setup.
- Each beat has an action, a feeling, something revealed, a camera intention and a sound or a deliberate silence.
- Wordless acting carries feeling through body language, face, timing and sound.

## Shots
- Shot ladder: wide, medium, close-up, insert on a hand or object, over-the-shoulder, reaction.
- Start a new space with a wide shot. Re-establish after a run of close-ups.
- Cutting between very different sizes of one subject is a shock cut. Step through a middle size unless the shock is the point.
- Match on action: cut in the middle of a movement.
- Eyeline: where a character looks sets the direction of the next shot.
- 180-degree rule: characters keep their screen sides within a scene.
- Mix lengths. Short shots for tension, long ones for feeling. Plan a 3-6 s median with a few 1-2 s inserts and some deliberate holds.
- Every shot has one clear focal action and a big readable silhouette.
- Move the camera because the story asks for it. Hold before and after a move. Rack focus only reads with shallow depth.

## Depth in 2D
- 4-6 layers: foreground silhouette, props, characters, set, far background, sky.
- Parallax .35 and .65 behind the action, 1 at the action, 1.5 in front.
- A big out-of-focus foreground shape in an over-the-shoulder shot is the cheapest strong depth cue.
- Blur by distance from the focus plane. In a close-up, use about 18 on the far layer and 12 on the near wall.

## Lens feel
Zoom below 1 and low blur for wide shots. Zoom 1.3-1.7 for mediums. Zoom 2.3-3.5 with heavy background blur for close-ups, which also suggests compressed space.

## Common failures
Too fast to read. Everything moving at once. Slow openings. Scenes that run long. Equal shot lengths. Dead air without a new image, turn or joke for more than 15 seconds.
