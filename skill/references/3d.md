# 3D route

3D uses real geometry and real cameras, drawn to match the flat 2D style. It must never look like a game or a glossy render. If a still and a 2D frame don't look like the same film, fix the 3D.

## How it stays on style
- Colours are the same sRGB hex values as 2D, with no tone mapping or scene lights.
- `mat()` gives flat colour, one shadow tone and a crisp warm rim edge. Never add MeshStandardMaterial, specular, bloom or real-time shadows.
- Sets come from `world3d.js`, and their walls are painted by the 2D set code, so `makeCafe` is the same room as `sets/cafe.js`.
- Contact shadows are soft blobs (`blob`) and lamp light is a faint painted cone, as in 2D.
- `post` applies the same blur, vignette, warm grade and grain as 2D.

## What 3D is for
Use it when the camera should move around the scene: a slow orbit, a push past a table, a door swinging toward the lens. Otherwise 2D is simpler.

## Toolkit
`new_film.py film --3d` copies the engine and installs three.js, so Node is needed. The starter film shows the patterns.
- `lib3d.js`: `Person` (`pos, yaw, lean, feet, hands, pole, hand, hy, hp, face`), characters `HERO, FRIEND, WOMAN, STRANGER`, `mat`, `mesh`, `gait`, `texture`.
- `world3d.js`: `makeCafe`, `makeStreet`, `table3`, `chair3`, `cup3`, `blob`, `pendant3`, and `walk3`, which places feet by distance along a path so they never slide.
- A film sets `window.renderAt`, `CUTF`, `DUR`, `EVENTS`, `PLANTS` and `READY`. Sound works as in 2D.

## Timing
Renders use software WebGL, about 1 frame per second on 2 cores, with one process. Each shot is its own file, so a stopped render resumes.

## Lessons
- Keep one world with fixed positions.
- Stills first: check the face reads from the camera side. Turn the head with `hy`.
- `sad` positive reads angry on the 3D face, the opposite of 2D, because of how the brows are built. Check on the first sheet.
- Keep the cup in front of the hand from the camera's side, so the sip reads.
- Choose a gun hand from the camera side. A character facing -x has their left on +z.
- A hat brim hides the eyes if the head pitches down.
- Check a bullet's nose direction on stills.
